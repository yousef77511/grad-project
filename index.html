About A7lam City

A7lam City is Egypt's most ambitious urban development project, designed to become the country's new political and financial hub. Located approximately 45 kilometers east of Cairo, this smart city represents a bold leap into the future of urban living.

With construction beginning in 2015, the city spans over 700 square kilometers in its first phase and is designed to eventually accommodate 6.5 million residents. The project represents a $58 billion investment in Egypt's future.
